Langas: Learn Latvian — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026

This policy explains what information the Android app Langas: Learn Latvian ("Langas", "the app", "we", "us") collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. Langas is a paid language-learning game that teaches Latvian to English speakers.

1. Information we collect

Langas signs you in only through Google Play Games. We do not ask for or store your email address, password, phone number, contacts, location, photos or any other personal files.

When you sign in, we receive and store:

  • Google Play Games player ID — a unique identifier used to recognise your account.
  • Google Play Games display name — your public gamer name.

When you use the app, we store:

  • Your best score and number of attempts for each exercise.
  • The date and time you created your account and last signed in.
  • A random session token that keeps you signed in. It is stored on our server and on your device.

Your answers are sent to our server only to calculate your score; individual answers are not stored.

2. Technical data

Like any online service, our server receives your device's IP address when the app connects to it. It is used only to deliver the service and to protect it from abuse (for example, rate limiting). Our web server may keep standard technical logs containing IP addresses for a limited time for security and troubleshooting.

3. How we use your information

  • To sign you in and keep your progress.
  • To calculate and save your scores.
  • To show the leaderboard.
  • To keep the service secure and working.

We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not sell or rent it to anyone.

4. Leaderboard

Your Google Play Games display name and total score are shown on the in-app leaderboard, where other Langas players can see them. Your player ID is never shown to other players.

5. No ads, no tracking

Langas contains no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking tools.

6. Purchases

Langas is sold through Google Play. Payments are processed entirely by Google. We never receive or store your payment card or billing details.

7. Third-party services

  • Google Play Games Services — sign-in. Google processes data under the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play — app distribution and payments.
  • Our hosting and database providers — store the data described above on our behalf.

8. Data security

All communication between the app and our server is encrypted with HTTPS. Access to the database is restricted to the developer.

9. Data retention and deletion

We keep your account data while your account exists. You can ask us to delete your account and all related data (player ID, display name, scores and sessions) at any time by emailing drysoftware1@gmail.com with the subject "Langas – delete my data" and your Google Play Games display name. We will complete the deletion within 30 days. Uninstalling the app removes the session token from your device but does not delete your data on our server.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, in the European Union under the GDPR), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your data, and to object to or restrict its processing. To use these rights, email us at the address below. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

11. Children

Langas is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has provided data to us, contact us and we will delete it.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The new version will be posted on this page with an updated date.

13. Contact

Questions or requests: drysoftware1@gmail.com
